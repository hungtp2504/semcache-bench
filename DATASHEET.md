# Datasheet — semcache-bench (draft, updated as the benchmark freezes)

Follows Gebru et al.'s "Datasheets for Datasets" structure. ▢ = filled at freeze.

## Motivation
Built to measure *structural* failures of similarity-threshold semantic caches:
the irreducible overlap between same-answer and different-answer query pairs, and
order-dependent cache behavior. Created by the "Cliffs and Slopes" project (paper
under submission to Information and Software Technology).

## Composition
- Query pairs (seed question, variant) with construction-derived expected labels
  (HIT: P1–P3; MISS: N1–N6) and dual LLM axis-compliance judgments.
- 46,214 pairs after judge filtering and MinHash deduplication; 5 domains,
  2 languages (EN, VI); per-seed contract: 12 HIT + up to 12 MISS (N4/N5
  overgenerated ×4, top-2 judge-validated kept — applicability is seed-conditional).
- Fields per record: see `config/record.schema.json`.

## Collection & generation process
- Seeds extracted from source documents by claude-sonnet-5; variants generated by
  claude-opus-5 (RQ6 subsets: sonnet/haiku); judged by two models with the primary
  judge always ≠ generator. No sampling control (subscription CLI); **complete
  generation logs** (full prompts, raw outputs, exact model strings, token usage,
  timestamps, prompt-version hashes) are included in `logs/`.
- Prompt versions: A4 v1.0 (pilot) → v1.2 (scale); judge rubric constant (v1.0).

## Recommended uses
Evaluating embedding models / cache architectures on answer-equivalence decisions;
studying order-dependence; calibration research. NOT a QA-accuracy benchmark: the
answers are grounded in specific source documents and may age.

## Known limitations
- LLM-generated text with LLM compliance filtering —
  no human anchor (the study's main limitation; see paper T3).
- N4/N5 applicability is seed-conditional (see paper §5, §8).
- Vietnamese domain drawn from legal/administrative register.
- Generator family is Claude-only (cross-vendor generation is future work).
